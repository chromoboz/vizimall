# VIZIMALL product sourcing rules

User instruction, 3 October 2026:
- Country storefronts are local supply markets. A Germany product must dispatch from verified stock in Germany to a customer in Germany. Apply the same rule to France, Netherlands, Poland, Spain, Portugal, Italy and Greece.
- Verify actual dispatch warehouse, stock, handling time and destination delivery estimate before listing. Brand nationality, manufacture location, a generic EU warehouse label or ability to ship to a country is insufficient proof of local dispatch.
- Target customer delivery is 3–6 days. Include handling/production when checking it and distinguish calendar and business days in the evidence and customer copy. Do not relabel slower products as 3–6 days.
- Multiple country tags require independently verified local fulfillment routes for each tagged country. Do not tag a China-dispatched product for all eight countries.
- Suppliers are not limited to CJ. Prefer free account/import/stock-sync arrangements. No paid subscriptions or inventory purchases without specific user authorization.
- Preserve the approved gallery, visual color/size choices, variant-photo switching and separate delivery/details layout.
- Next catalog batch target: 30–40 qualifying products total. Do not publish unverified products to reach the count.
