# VIZIMALL product sourcing rules

User instruction, 3 October 2026:
- Latest user rule (3 October 2026): cross-border supply is allowed if verified customer delivery to the destination is 3–7 days including handling. A product can use two or more country tags when each destination route qualifies. This supersedes the previous same-country dispatch restriction and 3–6 day limit.
- Verify actual dispatch warehouse, stock, handling time and destination delivery estimate before listing. Brand nationality, manufacture location, a generic EU warehouse label or ability to ship to a country is insufficient proof of local dispatch.
- Target customer delivery is 3–7 days. Include handling/production when checking it and distinguish calendar and business days in the evidence and customer copy. Do not relabel slower products as 3–7 days.
- Multiple country tags require independently verified fulfillment routes and delivery estimates for each tagged country. Do not blanket-tag an unverified product for all eight countries.
- Suppliers are not limited to CJ. Prefer free account/import/stock-sync arrangements. No paid subscriptions or inventory purchases without specific user authorization.
- Preserve the approved gallery, visual color/size choices, variant-photo switching and separate delivery/details layout.
- Latest catalog target (3 October 2026): 30–40 qualifying products per country's entire mall, across eight countries (240–320 country listings total). This replaces the earlier 30–40 total target. Do not publish unverified products to reach the count.
- Lifestyle was replaced by Kids & Play. Use store-kids for children's books, toys and children's room products; store-lifestyle is no longer an active route. The other five store tags remain unchanged.
