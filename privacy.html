Your privacy at VIZIMALL.

Website privacy information · 3 October 2026 · version 2026-10-03.1

Contact and your choices

VIZIMALL operates this website. For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, export or delete your data, email info@vizimall.com. We verify identity before releasing account information. You may also contact your local data protection authority. Business identity and full sales policies are being finalized separately; this page describes the website’s current data handling.

Essential shopping and security

Shopify handles sign-in, profiles, addresses, checkout, orders and payments. We retrieve your own account and orders to provide the services you request. Secure account tokens stay on the server; a secure HttpOnly session cookie lasts for at most 24 hours. The login binding cookie lasts 10 minutes. Signing in or buying does not give marketing consent.

Your bag and chosen country are stored in your browser for shopping; you can clear them through your browser. Your cookie choice is saved for up to 180 days. Your profile picture, favourites, reviews and support requests are held privately on Netlify. Names are saved in Shopify. Your profile picture is accessible only in your authenticated account; selecting a preset avatar removes your uploaded photo. Profile photos are resized and location metadata is removed. Approved review text, rating, display name and uploaded photos are public; purchase identifiers and account contact information are not published. Uploaded review photos are resized and location metadata is removed.

Necessary processing supports requested shopping, account access and support. Limited security rate counters use a hashed network address and are kept for no more than 48 hours; that address is not used in visit reports. Hosting and Shopify also process connection data according to their service policies.

Optional visit measurement

Measurement is off until you accept it. Rejecting does not block shopping. Use “Cookie preferences” at the foot of any page to change your choice or withdraw it. Accepting does not subscribe you to emails.

With consent, we count pageviews and consenting browser-tab sessions by day using a random session identifier held in session storage. We store only the page category, date, random event identifiers and counts. URL queries, referrers, account IDs, email addresses, raw IP addresses and device fingerprints are not included. We do not use advertising trackers or share this measurement with an advertising network.

Reports cover consenting sessions, not all visitors or unique people. A new tab, a new day or a blocked script can change totals. Measurement records expire after 31 days and are removed by daily cleanup. Withdrawal stops new measurement and clears the local measurement identifier. Existing measurement records stay separate from your account and expire under the retention period above.

Optional email updates

To subscribe, first verify the email you control through Shopify sign-in, then separately select the unchecked consent box for VIZIMALL news, product updates and offers. The email, consent wording/version, verification source and timestamp are saved as evidence of your choice. Cookies cannot reveal your email, and account creation, support requests and purchases do not subscribe you.

Use Email updates to subscribe or withdraw. Future marketing emails must include a working unsubscribe link. No campaign is sent by this website’s signup form. Active subscription records are kept while subscribed. After withdrawal, identifying subscription details are removed after 31 days; only an email hash and withdrawal date remain to prevent accidental re-subscription. Rejoining requires fresh explicit consent. Email lists are separate from website measurement and are not automatically shared with Shopify marketing tools.

Service providers and account records

Shopify provides the commerce and email-code sign-in service. Netlify provides hosting, server functions and private storage; VIZIMALL’s application storage is configured in Frankfurt, Germany. Provider operations can involve international processing under their applicable safeguards. See Shopify privacy information and Netlify privacy information.

Account favourites stay until removed or an account-deletion request is handled. Reviews stay until withdrawn or removed under moderation rules; withdrawing clears their photos. Support records stay while needed to handle your request. Transaction records kept in Shopify can be subject to accounting or dispute-retention obligations. Contact us to request deletion; necessary legal records may have to be retained. Full operating policies will be finalized before commercial launch.
